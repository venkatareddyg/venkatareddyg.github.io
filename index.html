Pitara

Family photos, sorted offline.

Pitara is an iPhone app that sorts your camera roll on the phone itself. It puts your family first, and clears the forwards, screenshots and look-alike shots you don't need. Your photos are never uploaded for sorting.

Backup is optional. If you turn it on, Pitara copies the photos you choose to your own Google Drive, and asks Google only for access to the files it creates. See the Privacy Policy.
